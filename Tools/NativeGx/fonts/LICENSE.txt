The tiny.ttf, cache.ttf and moves.ttf geometric test fonts and their shape definitions were authored for
wowdecomp's native font fixtures. They contain simple triangles, curves, a reflected component and blank glyphs,
and authored font programs (a one-instruction baseline and twilight-point movement
callbacks), not game font data or copied glyph designs. They are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0-1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

make_font_fixture.py uses fontTools only to regenerate these fixtures. fontTools
is not a client or CI runtime dependency. The committed fonts have deterministic
metadata and can be used directly by the hosted checks.
